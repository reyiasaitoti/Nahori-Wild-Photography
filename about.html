Nahori means a way of seeing the world: with patience, care, and a little more wonder than you arrived with.

The beginning

Made in Kenya, for the curious.

Nahori Wild Photography grew from a love of the small things: the pause before a lion moves, dust turning gold at dusk, the sound of a river you cannot yet see. We make safaris for people who want more than a beautiful itinerary. They want to be present inside it.

Our team brings together local knowledge, photographic instinct, and a deep respect for the communities and ecosystems that make each journey possible. That means honest advice, thoughtful logistics, and a lighter footprint wherever we can make one.

What guides us

Our promises are simple.

Go gently

We keep groups considered, routes intentional, and the wild in charge of the schedule.

Know the place

Our guides are storytellers and naturalists who know the land beyond the famous view.

Stay curious

Every guest sees differently. We listen closely and shape the journey around that.

Give back

We work with local partners and support the people who make Kenya's tourism future possible.
