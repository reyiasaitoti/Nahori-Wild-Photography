Why Nahori Wild

A good trip starts with listening.

Every traveller comes to the Mara with their own reasons. Maybe you have a camera you can’t wait to use. Maybe this is your first safari, or a trip you have been dreaming about for years. We start by asking what matters to you, then build a plan around your dates, group, and travel style.

We believe the best days leave room to watch and learn, not just tick off sightings. Before you commit, you’ll receive a clear itinerary and a quote that sets out the planned arrangements and costs.

How we like to travel

Simple things, done thoughtfully.

Take your time

Give wildlife and changing light the attention they deserve. The Mara sets the pace; no sighting or photograph can be guaranteed.

Make it personal

Tell us who is travelling, what you enjoy, and what would make the trip feel right. We’ll use that to shape a practical proposal.

Photography for all

Bring your camera, phone, or just your curiosity. Let us know what kind of photography support would be useful to you.

Be clear from the start

Your quote will explain what is included and excluded, along with the trip details, so you can make an informed decision.
