Out in the Mara

Good field days take patience.

Wildlife has its own plans. Our aim is to give you time to watch, learn, and make photographs without turning every drive into a race from one sighting to the next.

Before you travel, tell us what you enjoy photographing and how much guidance you would like. Bring the camera you have, or simply come ready to look closely. You do not need to be a professional to enjoy a photography-led safari.
