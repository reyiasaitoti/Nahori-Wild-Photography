Photography · In the field

Make space
for the shot.

From first light to the last frame, every detail of a Nahori photo safari is shaped around the way you want to see.

Why it works

More time.
Better stories.

We do not promise a list of sightings. We promise the conditions to notice what is unfolding: flexible departures, private vehicles, guides who understand animal behaviour, and days that do not end when the clock says they should.

Bring a phone, a film camera, or a full kit. Our job is to get you into position, keep the vehicle steady, and make sure there is enough quiet for the image to arrive.

Built around your eye

Choose your kind of fieldwork.

For curious travellers

The first frame

A gentle introduction to wildlife photography with simple guidance, space to experiment, and a guide who knows when to let the moment speak for itself.

For serious shooters

The long light

Extended time in the Mara, private vehicle access, low-density camps, and a photography-aware guide who can work with your pace and kit.

For families & groups

Many ways to see

A considered itinerary that gives young explorers, non-photographers, and keen shooters their own good days in the wild.

Optional add-on

After the safari

Extend the story with an edit session, image review, or a quiet portrait of your time here. Ask us what can be arranged.
