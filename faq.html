Planning a photography safari in the Mara? Here are answers to some of the questions guests often ask.

How much does a Maasai Mara safari cost?

There are no fixed package prices. The cost depends on your dates, group size, accommodation, transport, and what you would like included. Send us your details and we’ll prepare a quote for your trip.

What will my quote cover?

Your quote will set out the proposed accommodation and meal plan, guiding and game drives, park or conservancy fees, and transfers or flights if arranged. It will also state what is excluded, any additional costs to plan for, and the payment and cancellation terms for your trip.

How many days should I plan?

That depends on your schedule and what you hope to experience. A few nights gives you time to settle into the Mara; a longer stay can allow for a slower pace. Tell us how long you have and we can suggest an itinerary.

When should I visit?

The right time depends on your interests and dates. Seasonal conditions and wildlife movement vary, and sightings cannot be guaranteed. Share your preferred travel window and we’ll discuss what to expect.

Do I need to be a professional photographer?

Not at all. Photography-led safaris are for beginners, enthusiasts, experienced photographers, and curious travellers. Tell us how much camera guidance you’d enjoy, if any.

What camera equipment should I bring?

Bring the camera you are comfortable using. A telephoto lens can be useful for wildlife, but a phone or compact camera is welcome too. Once we know your plans, we can share practical packing suggestions.

Can non-photographers join?

Yes. Let us know who is travelling and what everyone enjoys. We’ll plan with the whole group in mind, not just the people carrying cameras.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp us on 0718 769 412. Share your dates, group size, preferred accommodation, and interests. We’ll follow up to discuss the details and prepare a tailored proposal.

What happens after I enquire?

We’ll get back to you to learn more about your plans. If the trip is a good fit, we’ll send an itinerary and a quote that sets out the proposed arrangements and costs for you to review.

Can you accommodate dietary or accessibility needs?

Please mention any dietary, mobility, medical, or other requirements when you enquire. We can discuss what may be possible with the accommodation and transport options included in your proposal.
